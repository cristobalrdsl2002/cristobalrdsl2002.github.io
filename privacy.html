Privacy Policy — Personal Automation

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Personal Automation is a personal tool used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

  • Data accessed: only the owner's own Google Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Apps Script and Calendar data, through Google's OAuth APIs.
  • Use: the data is used solely to run the owner's own automations, on their own computer.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Storage: the authorization token is stored locally on the owner's computer and never published.
  • Other users: the app is not offered to the public and collects no data from other people.

Personal Automation's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
